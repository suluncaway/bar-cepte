Quaker's Cocktail

Ordinary Drink · Alcoholic · Cocktail glass

Malzemeler

  • 3/4 oz Light rum
  • 3/4 oz Brandy
  • Juice of 1/4 Lemon
  • 2 tsp Raspberry syrup

Yapılışı

Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into a cocktail glass, and serve.
