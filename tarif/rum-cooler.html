Rum Cooler

Ordinary Drink · Alcoholic · Collins glass

Malzemeler

  • 2 oz light or dark Rum
  • 4 oz Lemon-lime soda
  • 1 Lemon

Yapılışı

Pour the rum and soda into a collins glass almost filled with ice cubes. Stir well and garnish with the lemon wedge.
