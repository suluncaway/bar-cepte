Hot Chocolate to Die for

Cocoa · Non alcoholic · Coffee mug

Malzemeler

  • 12 oz fine Chocolate
  • 1 tsp Butter
  • 1/2 tsp Vanilla extract
  • 1 cup Half-and-half
  • mini Marshmallows

Yapılışı

Melt the chocolate, butter and vanilla in a double boiler. When just smooth stir in the cream.
