Moscow Mule

Punch / Party Drink · Alcoholic · Copper Mug

Malzemeler

  • 2 oz Vodka
  • 2 oz Lime juice
  • 8 oz Ginger ale

Yapılışı

Combine vodka and ginger beer in a highball glass filled with ice. Add lime juice. Stir gently. Garnish.
