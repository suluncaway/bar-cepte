Ace

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Martini Glass

Malzemeler

  • 2 shots Gin
  • 1/2 shot Grenadine
  • 1/2 shot Heavy cream
  • 1/2 shot Milk
  • 1/2 Fresh Egg White

Yapılışı

Shake all the ingredients in a cocktail shaker and ice then strain in a cold glass.
