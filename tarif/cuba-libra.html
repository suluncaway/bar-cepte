Cuba Libra

Ordinary Drink · Alcoholic · Highball glass

Malzemeler

  • 1-2 shot Dark rum
  • Squeeze Lime
  • Fill with Coca-Cola
  • Ice

Yapılışı

Fill tall glass with ice cubes. Add rum. Rub cut edge of lime on rim of glass then squeeze juice into glass. Fill with Coca-Cola. Garnish with lime slice. Enjoy!
