Duchamp's Punch

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Old-fashioned glass

Malzemeler

  • 5 cl Pisco
  • 2.5 cl Lime Juice
  • 2.5 cl Pineapple Syrup
  • 1.5 cl St. Germain
  • 2 Dashes Angostura Bitters
  • Pinch Pepper
  • 2 sprigs Lavender

Yapılışı

Shake all ingredients. Double strain in a chilled double old fashioned glass with abig ice cube. Garnish with a couple of lavender sprigs
