Rum Milk Punch

Ordinary Drink · Alcoholic · Collins glass

Malzemeler

  • 2 oz Light rum
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 1 tsp Powdered sugar
  • Nutmeg

Yapılışı

Shake all ingredients (except nutmeg) with ice and strain into a collins glass. Sprinkle nutmeg on top and serve.
