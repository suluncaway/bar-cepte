Royal Flush

Shot · Alcoholic · Old-fashioned glass

Malzemeler

  • 1 1/2 oz Crown Royal
  • 1 oz Peach schnapps
  • 1/2 oz Chambord raspberry liqueur
  • 1 oz Cranberry juice

Yapılışı

Pour all the ingredients into tumbler over ice. Strain into glass.
