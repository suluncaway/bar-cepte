Orange Push-up

Ordinary Drink · Alcoholic · Hurricane glass

Malzemeler

  • 1.5 oz Spiced rum
  • 0.5 oz Grenadine
  • 4 oz Orange juice
  • 1 splash Sour mix

Yapılışı

Combine liquors in a blender. Add a half scoop of ice and blend. Garnish with an orange and cherry flag. So good it will melt in your mouth!!!
