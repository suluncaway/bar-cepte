Pure Passion

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Whiskey sour glass

Malzemeler

  • 40 ml Rum
  • 20 ml Passoa
  • 30 ml Lime Juice
  • 15 ml Passion fruit syrup
  • Dash Peach Bitters
  • Garnish with Mint

Yapılışı

Mix up all ingredients with a cocktail stirrer and serve with crushed ice with mint and edible flour if available.
