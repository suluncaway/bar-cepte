Brooklyn

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Cocktail glass

Malzemeler

  • 2 oz Rye Whiskey
  • 1 oz Dry Vermouth
  • 1/4 oz Maraschino Liqueur
  • 3 dashes Angostura Bitters
  • 1 Maraschino Cherry

Yapılışı

Combine ingredients with ice and stir until well-chilled. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
