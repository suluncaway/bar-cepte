Broadside

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Highball glass

Malzemeler

  • 1 shot 151 proof rum
  • 1/2 shot Scotch
  • 3 drops Bitters
  • 1 Fresh Wormwood
  • cubes Ice

Yapılışı

Half fill the glass with ice cubes. Crush the wormwood and add to ice. Pour rum, scotch and butters, then serve!
