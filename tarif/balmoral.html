Balmoral

Ordinary Drink · Alcoholic · Cocktail glass

Malzemeler

  • 1 1/2 oz Scotch
  • 1/2 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • 1/2 oz Dry Vermouth
  • 2 dashes Bitters

Yapılışı

In a mixing glass half-filled with ice cubes, combine all of the ingredients. Stir well. Strain into a cocktail glass.
