Sangria

Punch / Party Drink · Alcoholic · Pitcher

Malzemeler

  • 1 bottle Red wine
  • 1/2 cup Sugar
  • 1 cup Orange juice
  • 1 cup Lemon juice
  • Cloves
  • Cinnamon

Yapılışı

Mix all together in a pitcher and refrigerate. Add cloves and cinnamon sticks to taste. Serve in wine glasses.
