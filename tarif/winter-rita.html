Winter Rita

Beer · Alcoholic · Highball Glass

Malzemeler

  • 1 2/3 oz Tequila
  • 1/4 oz Campari
  • 3/4 oz Lime Juice
  • 1/2 oz Orange Juice
  • 1/2 oz Rosemary Syrup
  • Dash Salt

Yapılışı

Salt rim. Combine all ingredients, shake with ice, and strain over fresh ice.⠀
