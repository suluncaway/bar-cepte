747 Drink

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Highball glass

Malzemeler

  • 1 oz Vodka
  • 1 oz Roses sweetened lime juice
  • 1 oz Cranberry Juice
  • Top Sprite

Yapılışı

Fill a Collins glass with ice. Pour in vodka, lime cordial, and cranberry juice. Fill up with Sprite. Garnish with a Lime wheel or some cranberries
