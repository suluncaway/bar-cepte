Barracuda

Ordinary Drink · Alcoholic · Margarita glass

Malzemeler

  • 4.5 cl Rum
  • 1.5 cl Galliano
  • 6 cl Pineapple Juice
  • 1 dash Lime Juice
  • top up Prosecco

Yapılışı

Shake pour ingredients with ice. Strain into glass, top with Sparkling wine.
