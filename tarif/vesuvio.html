Vesuvio

Ordinary Drink · Alcoholic · Old-fashioned glass

Malzemeler

  • 1 oz Light rum
  • 1/2 oz Sweet Vermouth
  • Juice of 1/2 Lemon
  • 1 tsp Powdered sugar
  • 1 Egg white

Yapılışı

Shake all ingredients with ice, strain into an old-fashioned glass over ice cubes, and serve.
