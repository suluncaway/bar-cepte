Espresso Rumtini

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Cocktail glass

Malzemeler

  • 1 shot Rum
  • 1/2 shot Vanilla syrup
  • 1 shot Espresso
  • 1 shot Coffee

Yapılışı

Mix together in a cocktail glass. Garnish with some choclate powder and coffee beans
