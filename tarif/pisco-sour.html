Pisco Sour

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Cocktail glass

Malzemeler

  • 2 oz Pisco
  • 1 oz Lemon juice
  • 1-2 tblsp Sugar
  • 1 Ice
  • Egg White

Yapılışı

Vigorously shake and strain contents in a cocktail shaker with ice cubes, then pour into glass and garnish with bitters.
