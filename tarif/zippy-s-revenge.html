Zippy's Revenge

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Old-fashioned glass

Malzemeler

  • 2 oz Amaretto
  • 2 oz Rum
  • 4 oz Grape Kool-Aid

Yapılışı

Mix Kool-Aid to taste then add Rum and ammaretto. shake well to disolve the sugar in the Kool-Aid... serve cold
