Jitterbug

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Cocktail Glass

Malzemeler

  • 2 jiggers Gin
  • 1 jigger Vodka
  • 3 dashes Grenadine
  • 1 shot Lime juice
  • Around rim put 1 pinch Sugar
  • 3 dashes Sugar syrup
  • Fill to top with Soda water

Yapılışı

Wet glass, dip rim in sugar. Then add Ice. Then add everything else. It's that simple!
