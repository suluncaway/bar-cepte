New York Lemonade

Cocktail · Alcoholic · Cocktail glass

Malzemeler

  • 2 oz Absolut Citron
  • 1 oz Grand Marnier
  • 2 oz sweetened Lemon juice
  • 1 oz Club soda

Yapılışı

Serve in a chilled cocktail glass. Lemon and sugar the rim. Stir and Strain.
