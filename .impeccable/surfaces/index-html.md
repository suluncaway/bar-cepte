## Direction contract
THESIS: The bar as Turkish matchbox-and-coaster print ephemera; refuses the near-black-with-amber-glow moody bar default.
OWN-WORLD: Deep ink ground (blue-black, not neutral), two-color flat print: each cocktail family owns a coaster hue (citrus yellow, sour cherry, mint, night blue, clay); paper-cream ink for text; no glow, no glass, no gradients; flat fills, thick 2px keylines, round coaster discs.
STORY: Visitor sees their bar, sees what they can make now, sees one buy-this-unlock-more suggestion, opens a recipe, makes it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Standard top bar (logo, search, account); tab row; "Şu an yapabileceklerin" count as a big condensed numeral; cocktail cards in a grid with round coaster stamp for makeable state; primary action in citrus yellow.
FORM: candidate 3 of 7 (matchbox & coaster ephemera), seed:0ad9b05af033cb15. Raises: measures in a tabular mono-numeric column (from Ikeda); strict type scale, rank by weight and case (from timetable); one hue per section (from acetate manual). Signature move: round coaster stamp for "Yapabilirsin" + matchstick strip for "+X tarif".
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
